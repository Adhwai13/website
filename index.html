About Us

The Department of Civil Engineering started in the year 1993 and is the first department of the college. It prepares students to plan, design and construct buildings, roads, bridges and water supply systems.

The department carries out soil testing and material testing for local government projects. New: a Surveying Camp is conducted every year for third-year students.

Academic Programmes

Programmes offered: B.Tech Civil Engineering (4 years) and M.Tech Structural Engineering (2 years).

Important subjects in the curriculum:

  • Surveying
  • Strength of Materials
  • Structural Analysis
  • Geotechnical Engineering
  • Transportation Engineering
  • Environmental Engineering
